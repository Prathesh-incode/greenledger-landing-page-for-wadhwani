What GreenLedger offers

Make responsible habits easier to track.

A simple desktop-app prototype focused on household accountability and community awareness.

🗂️

Waste tracking

Log biodegradable and non-biodegradable waste and review household history.

🌱

Green Scores & credits

View a Green Score and credits designed to make better segregation habits engaging.

🏆

Community participation

Use community views and leaderboards to encourage shared responsibility.

How it works

Small steps. Better habits.

STEP 01

Separate

Keep biodegradable and non-biodegradable waste apart at home.

STEP 02

Record

Log the waste in GreenLedger and review your household progress.

STEP 03

Improve together

Use scores, credits, and community views to stay motivated.

Our mission

Cleaner homes. Greener communities.

GreenLedger aims to support responsible waste management, one household at a time.
